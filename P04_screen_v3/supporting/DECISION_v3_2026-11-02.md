## A. The dispatch decisions (quoted; dispatch, under Mahmood's delegation, 2026-11-02)

> 1. N_S = 5 (Stage S at most 5 full screens). This matches FIELD_USE_CASE_PROTOCOL.md line 16, "Screen up to 5 candidates", which you cite but did not flag as conflicting with N_S=10. The field protocol stays unchanged and byte-identical.
> 2. Removing rows with the offline metadata/title filter is a DECLARED filter stage. The run reports its counts per rule; nothing is hidden. Removed rows do not use a Stage F slot. They are not visits; they are reported separately with counts.
> 3. Licence: CC BY or CC0 only. CC BY-NC-SA is OUT (consistency and reuse).
> 4. Effort formula and constants: accepted as drafted. Add an explicit disclosure that the calibration rests on a single human estimate (4.0 h).
> 5. N_F = 40, Stage F cap 20 min, N_S = 5, Stage S cap 60 min.
> 6. OD-13 is resolved: the P04-screen-v2 Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23883314 was verified by the coordinator (doi.org resolves to the Zenodo record, and its title and version match the tag). Cite it as verified. The v2 run receipt's PENDING stays as the historical value used at frame-build time.
> 7. EVERY other remaining OD item: settle it by the STRICTER reading, meaning the one that makes a candidate less likely to pass and leaves the screener less discretion. This includes forest-plot-row k counting, data-availability statements in Stage F and Stage F early stop.

**Drafter's note on decision 1.** The draft's N_S = 10 conflicted with the field protocol's "Screen up to 5 candidates". The draft did not flag this conflict, and it should have. v3 now uses 5.

**Drafter's note on decision 3.** "CC BY or CC0 only" also removes CC BY-SA and CC BY-NC, which v2 named as acceptable. This is tighter than v2 and is recorded in CHANGES_v2_to_v3.md row 17.
