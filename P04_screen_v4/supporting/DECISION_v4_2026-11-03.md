## The coordinator's judgement on #18 (made and recorded before any v4 screening)
**#18 (10.1002/dmrr.3670) is not added to v4 Stage S.** There are three reasons.

1. **Another record failed on the identical ground.** v3 queue order 33 (10.3389/fendo.2025.1614597, an updated growth-factor NMA for diabetic foot ulcers) failed F6 for the same reason as #18. Its nodes are generic growth factors (EGF, PDGF, VEGF, FGF and others) that correspond to substances with INNs but are not named by INN. Its v3 F-log entry carries the same ambiguity flag. A looser reading cannot be applied to #18 alone without treating two identically situated records differently.
2. **Applying it consistently breaks the fixed cap.** Applied to every record it affects, the looser reading would add both #18 and #33. Together with the 4 unscreened passers that makes 6 full screens, above the Stage S cap of 5 fixed on 2026-11-02 and kept unchanged for v4.
3. **The reading would be chosen after the outcome was known.** v3's open items were settled by the stricter reading before screening. Changing the drug-node rule now, after the v3 Stage F outcomes are known, would let the result of a screen decide its rules.

v4 therefore screens the 4 remaining v3 Stage F passers and nothing else. The F6 rule stays exactly as released in v3.
