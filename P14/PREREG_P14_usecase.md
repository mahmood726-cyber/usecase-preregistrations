## Prespecified question

Across the prespecified sample of paediatric RCT meta-analyses, how often was the cumulative evidence statistically conclusive under the fixed rule below before the final included trial report, and how many included trials and participants followed that point? Separately, where recruitment dates establish it, how many trials actually started after evidence was available? Later publication alone cannot establish that a trial was initiated unnecessarily.

## Sampling and eligibility

This is a meta-research pilot across journals, not a sample restricted to the target journal. The database frame is Europe PMC, English-language records marked OPEN_ACCESS:Y, with first publication from 2017-01-01 through 2025-12-31 inclusive. These are complete calendar years within ten years of the planned 2026-10-27 search. No significance, effect magnitude, heterogeneity, citation, journal prestige, or harness availability filter is permitted. General biomedical journals are included.

Include published, human, conventional pairwise meta-analyses of randomized treatment comparisons, with **10 <= k <= 60 independent trials in the single designated synthesis**. The protocol's example of five trials does not override its explicit minimum of ten for these methods. Require a freely readable full text, operationalised as an open-access full text retrievable from Europe PMC (fullTextXML, with figures and supplements via its supplementaryFiles service) without payment, login or circumvention of access controls. There is no licence requirement (amendment AM-S1b): the reuse licence of each review is recorded, not used as an eligibility criterion. No paywall workarounds or author contact.

Require printed per-study estimates and standard errors/95% CIs, or arm-level numbers sufficient to reconstruct them, from the review's forest plot, table, or supplement. Each trial, outcome, contrast, follow-up, effect scale, and original pooling model must be identifiable. No digitization of bar widths, Kaplan-Meier reconstruction, borrowing missing outcomes, or fetching new trial-level datasets. Require the original pooled estimate and CI. Accept RR, OR, HR on the log scale and MD or SMD on their original analysis scales. Require positive finite sampling variances.

For this pilot, restrict the designated original pooling method to conventional inverse-variance fixed effect or inverse-variance random effects using DL or REML, with identifiable Wald or Hartung-Knapp inference and any variance modification explicitly documented. Exclude unidentified or unsupported estimators, Bayesian/network/multilevel/multivariate models, diagnostic/prognostic/surrogacy analyses, individual-patient analyses without a reproducible aggregate synthesis, nonrandomized or inseparably mixed designs, protocols, abstracts only, retractions, and irreducibly dependent effects. Cluster or crossover trials qualify only with the original appropriate adjusted estimates/variances available. Multi-arm trials qualify only when the designated synthesis has one independent contrast per trial, using the original documented arm combination. Splitting shared controls does not itself establish independence.

Designate exactly one synthesis per review before extraction: the first explicitly named primary outcome in Methods; otherwise the first efficacy outcome listed in Methods. Within it, use the authors' primary comparison and primary follow-up; if unspecified, use their first displayed overall forest plot for that outcome, then its first comparison and time point in document order. For co-primary outcomes use Methods order. Use the corrected version of the paper. If that designated synthesis fails eligibility, exclude the review; do not search its secondary outcomes for a qualifying or more dramatic result.

Deduplicate DOI (lowercase, strip DOI URL prefixes), then PMID, then normalized title + first author + year. Merge versions and corrections. For explicitly linked updates of the same review question retain only the latest eligible publication in the window, breaking date ties by canonical identifier. Independent reviews of overlapping trials remain separate; identify overlap later and do not imply independent participant totals.

Before the eventual sample is locked, check novelty in PubMed and Google Scholar with the review's exact title and DOI combined with the paper-specific method terms below. Exclude an already published equivalent reanalysis of the same designated synthesis, including one in the original report. Routine related methods alone do not establish duplication. Record the source and reason without running a new analysis. This check is prospective here; no individual review is selected now.

P14 population: children from birth through 17 years at trial entry; a separately published paediatric-only pooled analysis qualifies only if it is the review's designated primary synthesis. Mixed adult/child results without that separation are excluded. Trials of caregiver interventions qualify when the designated outcome is measured in the child. Require publication years for every included trial report, spanning at least five years (latest minus earliest >= 5), and a unique participant count per trial for the designated comparison. Trials may be older than 2017; the review publication window does not constrain trial years.

Recruitment start dates are sought only in the review's tables/supplements. Their absence does not exclude the main cumulative analysis, but leaves the literal count of trials started after the evidence point incomplete and explicitly bounded. The primary publication-based tally must never be relabelled as a verified tally of later trial starts.

## Fixed conclusiveness and chronology rule

Use **sustained exclusion of the null by the two-sided 95% CI at the original model**, not a trial-sequential-analysis boundary. The null is zero for MD/SMD/log-ratio scales (one after exponentiating a ratio). Use the same documented original effect metric, pooling model, variance estimator, CI method and corrections at every cumulative step; re-estimate tau-squared at each step if random effects. Do not switch fixed/random models in response to a running heterogeneity statistic.

Group trial effects by the publication year of the report supplying the selected outcome. Add all reports from the same year together, so arbitrary within-year ordering cannot create an early crossing. Use the report year available in the review; do not backdate final outcomes to an earlier interim report. Missing or ambiguous required report years fail chronology eligibility. Evaluate only year-end steps containing at least two independent trials. Let J be the last year block and j* the earliest block j < J such that at every block j through J the 95% CI lies strictly on the same side of the null. Require at least one later year block. A CI touching the null does not pass. Undefined model fits or boundary uncertainty make that sequence indeterminate; do not skip an undefined step.

The criterion corresponds to sustained two-sided p < 0.05 when the test and CI are equivalent. It is a retrospective operational definition of statistical conclusiveness, not proof of clinical importance, equivalence, prospective stopping validity, or ethical waste. It has no repeated-testing correction and is not TSA. A final nonsignificant synthesis cannot have a sustained crossing; count it as no early conclusiveness rather than exclude it. A transient crossing followed by null inclusion is not conclusive.

Define T as 31 December of the crossing year. Report:
- early-conclusiveness indicator and year;
- K_later = number of included independent trial reports in strictly later years, and N_later = sum of their comparison-specific randomized participant counts, each participant counted once within a trial;
- years between crossing and final included trial report;
- among later reports, K_started and N_started for trials documented in the review/supplement to have begun recruitment after T. A recruitment start year strictly greater than the crossing year establishes this; a same-year start does not. Dates known only imprecisely and overlapping T are unknown. Studies already recruiting by T are not counted as later starts, even if they ended after T.

For missing start dates, give bounds [documented later-start trials, documented later-start trials + unknown-start later-report trials], with analogous participant bounds. Do not impute dates or assign zero to unknowns. Counts concern only trials included in the source review, not all trials ever conducted. A trial entirely initiated after T is the only basis for assigning all of its randomized participants to N_started. Partial recruitment after T is not inferred from total trial N.

Sensitivity analyses, fixed here: (a) require the first passing step to contain at least ten trials instead of two; (b) leave primary classifications intact but show counts at least two calendar years after the crossing year, as a descriptive lag check. No search for a cutoff producing a larger waste claim.

## Mandatory gates and reuse

All steps in this section are future work, not work performed for this draft.

1. **AI double extraction with adjudication.** Extraction is performed entirely by AI models; no person extracts data. Two models extract independently from the same source version (the version of record PDF; if the Europe PMC rendering of that same version is used, record which), without seeing each other's output: Codex (OpenAI) and agy running Claude (Anthropic) or Gemini (Google), with the model name and version recorded. Compare all trial identities, contrasts, outcomes, time points, effect scales, arm sizes, numerical inputs, model settings and any chronology fields. Report initial exact field-level agreement and the number of discrepant fields. A third model, from a family used by neither extractor (Gemini if agy ran Claude; Claude if agy ran Gemini), adjudicates every discrepant field against the source PDF. It sees the two candidate values unlabelled as to which extractor produced them, and must cite the page and figure/table for its value. It may return a third value only with such a citation. Final values must be complete for every required field at source precision. An unresolved required field excludes the review. Agreement percentages never excuse a material unresolved discrepancy. The reproduction gate (2) is the main correctness check on the extracted set.
2. **Original-model reproduction.** Reproduce the original designated pooled point estimate AND both 95% CI limits using its own documented model, tau-squared estimator, CI method, corrections and handling of trial arms. On the published scale, each value must differ by no more than half the last displayed decimal unit: tolerance = 0.5 * 10^(-d) + 1e-12 for a number printed to d decimal places (equivalent last-place tolerance for scientific notation). For ratios compare exponentiated results. Do not use a percentage tolerance or widen it after failure. If higher-precision source data exist, use them. Require agreement of significance/direction; rounding that leaves a boundary unresolved is indeterminate, not a pass. Identify a transcription or documented implementation error once through adjudication, then rerun the same specification. If reproduction still fails, exclude and report the reason and discrepancies. Do not tune the estimator to force agreement.
3. **App/R agreement.** Only after gates 1-2 may the new method run in the app and independently in R using the package employed in that app paper's validation. **Pinned implementation (2026-10-28).** App: allmeta `cumulative-subgroup/` at merge commit **`0dd16ee676da6334b177dce39226e5ffe925bafe`** on `main` (pull request #278, merged 2026-10-28T08:12:36Z after all CI passed). Pinned files: `index.html` sha256 `6ff010e022acf4aed30bde48635d89bbbc4af7f061e61ac7fec58401b1ac4007`; R reference `validate/p14-reference.json` sha256 `b7b3a71435636c73c65077f733684f69388fc7eb4d9f24958e57004e8b847771`. The merged opt-in panel implements this document's rule:
- FE-IV, DL or REML random effects;
- Wald or Hartung-Knapp CIs;
- report-year blocks, with evaluation only once a block holds at least 2 trials;
- strict sustained exclusion of the null, with INDETERMINATE for undefined steps;
- K/N later and sensitivity (a).
The earlier PM-only study-by-study view is unchanged. R reference: R 4.6.0 with metafor 5.2-1, the version in the app's validation manifest; the CRAN Windows binary reports being built under R 4.6.1. Validation:
- 41 fixture cases match the app to 1e-9 (tau-squared 1e-6), with categorical outputs exact; 32/32 tests pass.
- Rebuilt under metafor 5.2-1, the reference is numerically identical to the 5.0.1 build (maximum difference 0).
- An independent re-derivation of the decision rule from the reference finds 0 mismatches.
- CI on the merged head: the Playwright suite passed, and shared-tests passed 2112 with 4 skipped. A flaky pixels test in an unrelated app (limit-ma) failed once and passed on rerun, unchanged. No silent method substitution if the app lacks the specified estimator. Require finite estimates and CI endpoints to agree within 1e-8 * (1 + absolute R value), p-values within 1e-6, and all categorical decisions to match. Failure is a reported implementation failure and unresolved result, never silently a robust conclusion.

**Human verification (pre-specified, capped; AM-H2 replaces AM-H1's scope on 2026-10-28).** People check AI-extracted values against the source; they do not extract. Checkers are co-authors of this paper, including Mahmood, recorded by initials and date for every row checked. A **row** is one trial's record in the designated synthesis (all required fields). A row is in error if any required field differs from the source at source precision, or if trial identity, arm, outcome, time point or effect scale is mapped wrongly.
- **Humans check:** (a) every row of each review in the headline count (A: early-conclusive reviews); and (b) a seeded 5% sample of all other rows: ceil(0.05 x rows) per review, minimum 1 row per review. Rows are taken in ascending order of the lowercase hex SHA-256 of UTF-8 `2026102714-HV|P14|<review canonical key>|<row index in source order>|<trial label as printed>`, ties by that string. Seed **2026102714-HV**.
- **Discrepant fields go to AIs first.** A field on which the two extractors disagreed, and which the third model (Claude) resolved against the source, is re-checked by a **fourth AI pass**. That pass is agy running a model family different from both its own extractor role (Google Gemini) and the adjudicator (Anthropic): GPT-OSS 120B, recorded by name and version. It sees the source and the adjudicated value, and must confirm the value with a citation or contradict it. Only fields on which the AIs still disagree after the fourth pass go to a human. If agy cannot run that model headless and reliably, as tested on a synthetic fixture before first use, those fields go to a human instead. They are never skipped.
- **Escalation:** if the human-found error rate in a review's sample (b) is above 2% (rows in error / rows sampled), that review receives a **full AI re-extraction** (both extractors, compare, adjudication and the fourth pass again) plus a **human check of its headline rows**. It does not receive a full human check.
- **Packets and load.** Work is split into packets of at most about 30 minutes, estimated at 1.5 minutes per row, so about 20 rows. Each co-author receives at most one packet per week. Packets are spread round-robin across all co-authors of this paper, Mahmood included, and each request states the packet's estimated row count. A packet not returned within 7 days is **reassigned** to the next available co-author, never skipped. Assignment, send date, return date and reassignment are logged.
- After any human correction, gates 2-3 and the analysis for that review are rerun with the corrected data. The corrected result is reported as the result, and the before/after values are listed in Extended data. A correction that makes the review fail the reproduction gate excludes it, reported as such. Headline rows (a) can be checked only after the analysis; their corrections are applied in the same way, and any reclassification is reported.

**Reported in Extended data:** for each review, rows checked by humans in (a) and (b); fields resolved by the third model and confirmed or contradicted by the fourth AI pass; fields sent to humans; human-found errors; the pooled human-found error rate in sample (b) with a Wilson 95% interval, and per review; escalations; packets sent, returned and reassigned; each checker's initials and dates. Methods wording: **"AI double extraction with human verification"**. The process is never described as human extraction.

Harness data are optional assistance, never a sampling frame. Reuse a row only for meta-analysis X when it is verified against X's own forest plot and exactly matches X's included trial, arm contrast, population, outcome definition, time point, analysis population and effect metric. Check the full trial membership as well as every reused numerical field. Record harness path/version/hash and original source location. A row originating from meta-analysis X is used only to reanalyse X, even if another review includes that trial. A trial name or topic match alone is insufficient. All other rows are independently AI double-extracted (with adjudication and human verification as above) from X's forest plot/table/supplement. Reused rows still require independent source checking and the same agreement and reproduction gates. No harness trial data have been opened or reused in this lane.

## Outputs, denominators, and new versus robust

Headline metric: A/M evaluable meta-analyses with sustained early conclusiveness, with exact counts and percentage. Also report A/S selected, unresolved cases U, and descriptive missing-result bounds A/S to (A+U)/S; gate exclusions are a separate attrition category, not assumed negative. Give medians and ranges of K_later/N_later and K_started/N_started among early-conclusive reviews, and the number with adequate start-date evidence. If M=0, report not estimable. Do not pool clinical effect sizes across different questions or attach an independence-based field confidence interval to overlapping reviews.

One summary figure: a review-by-year timeline marking the first sustained crossing, final publication year, later report counts and verified later starts, with unresolved rows visibly labelled. Extended-data table/archive: every selected review and exclusion, citation/DOI, designated outcome, k, source/model settings, agreement and reproduction status, crossing status/year, cumulative year-block results, K/N later, K/N started and missing-date bounds, overlap flags, novelty assessment and provenance. Sum totals across reviews only as trial/participant appearances; also report deduplicated trial totals where identity permits, never claim these are unique persons across reviews.

Final journal text: 150-250 words plus one summary figure, all individual results in Extended data and the archive. Headline template: "In A of M reproducible paediatric meta-analyses, the cumulative 95% interval first excluded the null and remained so before the last trial report; later reports accounted for K trial appearances and N participant appearances. Recruitment dates verified X trials began after that point; Y had unknown timing." Numbers remain blank here.

"New" means an unreported sustained-evidence timing/count result for the designated synthesis, with any prior equivalent analysis excluded at the novelty stage. It can be informative whether early conclusiveness is present or absent. "Robust" means no qualifying early cutoff under this prespecified rule, or a reported conclusion retained under the fixed sensitivity checks; label these separately. An unresolved analysis is neither new nor robust. Do not call later studies unnecessary solely because statistical significance had been reached.

Independence is **not met** if any normalised label repeats or any such annotation is present. It **stays unresolved** if the plot cannot be read, or the designated rows cannot be identified. *Clarification added 2026-10-28 while the pass was running, in the strict direction and applied to every record:* independence counts as met only when one label was transcribed per designated trial (number of labels = k_designated). A partial row list cannot show that no trial repeats. The trigger was one record (P14 rank 506), where 8 labels were transcribed for k = 12; it stays unresolved.
  3. A record whose independence is met is re-decided by the same `decide.py`, including the stage-D per-study and model checks. Every other criterion is unchanged.
  All other UNRESOLVED records, and any record the pass cannot settle, remain not eligible. Seeded order and the 30-review cap are unchanged. ## Freeze and future release

Before any future trial extraction: complete the full database search and eligibility/novelty log; retain the specified rules unless a dated amendment is needed; pin the app and validation-package versions; freeze the seed, frame and method document; record its SHA-256 and UTC time; and follow the protocol's GitHub release plus Zenodo DOI procedure. The server-side release must exist before extraction, and its Zenodo archival DOI is to be recorded. No release or deposition is made in this draft-only task. Registration changes must precede extraction and explain the change; feasibility limitations do not authorize outcome switching. Preserve the paper's existing use case.

The final report must state all attrition, gate failures, missingness and method limitations, report whatever result occurs, and make no clinical recommendations or priority/ranking claims.
